# StockFlow

Inventory, sales and shop accounts for shops, wholesalers and distributors in Bangladesh. Android, Windows and the web, in Bangla and English. Sells without internet and syncs when you are back online.

## Features

- **Sell fast:** scan or search, cash / bKash / Nagad / Rocket / bank / card, split payments, discounts, dues with WhatsApp and SMS reminders, held sales, customer search and quick add, price tiers
- **Bills and papers:** Mushak 6.3 invoices on 58 mm, 80 mm, A4, A5, Letter or a custom size; quotations; delivery challans with partial deliveries and proof of delivery
- **Stock:** low-stock alerts, batches and expiry, serial/IMEI numbers, stock counts, transfers between branches, barcode labels, used and second-hand items
- **Buying:** supplier bills, purchase orders, part deliveries and returns, supplier statements and dues
- **Money:** cash drawer per branch, bank and mobile wallets, closing the day, expenses, lenders and borrowers with interest, profit and sales reports
- **People:** customers and suppliers with full details and statements, staff pages with attendance, roles and permissions, PIN and fingerprint lock
- **Shop:** multiple branches with their own invoice codes and receipt text, workshop services, light and dark mode
