# StockFlow

**দোকানের হিসাব, স্টক আর বিক্রি — এক অ্যাপে।**
Inventory, sales and shop accounts for shops, wholesalers and distributors in
Bangladesh. Android, Windows and the web browser, in Bangla and English.
Sells without internet and syncs when you are back online.

This repository holds the released builds. The source code is private.

## Getting started

1. Install StockFlow on the till computer or phone.
2. Sign up with your email. Your shop starts a **15-day free trial** straight away.
3. Add your products through **Purchase** (scan the barcode or type them in),
   then start selling from **Sell**.
4. Add staff from **All sections › Roles**; each person signs in with their own
   account and sees only what their role allows.
5. Before the trial ends, contact StockFlow to extend your shop's licence.

## What StockFlow does

- **Sell fast:** scan or search, cash / bKash / Nagad / Rocket / bank / card,
  split payments, discounts, dues (baki khata) with WhatsApp and SMS reminders
- **Bills:** Mushak 6.3 invoices on 58 mm, 80 mm or A4, auto-print, PDF to share
- **Stock:** low-stock alerts, batches and expiry, serial/IMEI numbers,
  stock counts, transfers between branches, barcode labels
- **Buying:** supplier bills, purchase orders by WhatsApp or PDF, part
  deliveries and returns, supplier dues
- **Money:** cash drawer per branch, bank and mobile wallets, close the day,
  expenses, profit and sales reports
- **Shop:** multiple branches, staff roles and permissions, PIN and fingerprint
  lock, light and dark mode, Bangla and English

## Updates

StockFlow checks this repository for new versions. On Windows and Android open
**Settings › Check for updates** and install; the web app offers a reload.
Every update is signed, and the app refuses one that is not.

## Your data

Each shop's data is kept on its own devices and in the shop's cloud account,
separate from every other shop. Signing out of the web app on a shared computer
removes the shop's data from that browser.
