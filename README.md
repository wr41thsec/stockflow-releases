# StockFlow

Inventory, sales and shop accounts for shops, wholesalers and distributors in Bangladesh. Android, Windows and the web, in Bangla and English. Sells without internet and syncs when you are back online.

## Features

- **Sell fast:** scan or search, cash / bKash / Nagad / Rocket / bank / card, split payments, discounts, dues with WhatsApp and SMS reminders in your own wording, held sales, customer search and quick add, price tiers
- **Bills and papers:** Mushak 6.3 invoices on 58 mm, 80 mm, A4, A5, Letter or a custom size; quotations; delivery challans with partial deliveries and proof of delivery
- **Stock:** low-stock alerts, batches and expiry, serial/IMEI numbers, stock counts, transfers between branches, used and second-hand items, bundles (combos), dead-stock report
- **Barcode labels:** Code-128, EAN-13 and QR, roll and A4 sheets, live preview, print or PDF
- **Buying:** supplier bills, purchase orders, part deliveries and returns, freight and duty added to item cost, supplier statements and dues
- **Money:** cash drawer per branch, bank and mobile wallets, closing the day, expenses, lenders and borrowers with interest, store credit on refunds, profit, VAT, category and due-ageing reports
- **People:** customers and suppliers with full details and statements, staff pages with attendance, roles and permissions, PIN and fingerprint lock
- **Shop:** multiple branches with their own invoice codes and receipt text, workshop services, switch sections on or off, light and dark mode
