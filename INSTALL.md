# How to install StockFlow

**English** | [বাংলা](INSTALL-bn.md)

Read slowly, one step at a time. You do not need to know anything about computers. If you get stuck, ask a family member or a friend to sit with you for 10 minutes. Everything here is free to try for 15 days.

**You need:** a phone or a computer, and an internet connection (only the first time).

Pick your device:

- [Android phone](#android)
- [Windows computer](#windows)
- [Use it in the browser (no install)](#in-the-browser)
- [Create your account](#create-your-account)
- [If something goes wrong](#if-something-goes-wrong)

---

## Android

1. **Open the download link on your phone.** Tap this: [StockFlow.apk](https://github.com/wr41thsec/stockflow-releases/releases/latest/download/StockFlow.apk). It is a big file, so use Wi-Fi if you can.
2. **Wait for the download to finish.** You will see a message at the top of the screen. If the phone asks "Download this type of file?", tap **Download** or **OK**.
3. **Tap the file.** Pull down the top of the screen and tap **StockFlow.apk**. Or open **Files** (or **My Files**), then **Downloads**, and tap **StockFlow.apk**.
4. **The phone may say "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source".**
   - Tap **Settings**.
   - Switch on **Allow from this source**.
   - Press the **Back** button.
   This is normal. It only means the app did not come from the Play Store.
5. **Tap Install.** Wait a few seconds.
6. **If you see "Play Protect" or "Blocked by Play Protect":** tap **More details**, then tap **Install anyway**.
7. **Tap Open.** StockFlow opens. Next, [create your account](#create-your-account).

Later, you will find the **StockFlow** icon on your phone like any other app.

---

## Windows

You need Windows 10 or Windows 11.

1. **Open the download link** in your browser (Chrome, Edge, anything): [StockFlow-Setup.exe](https://github.com/wr41thsec/stockflow-releases/releases/latest/download/StockFlow-Setup.exe).
2. **Wait for the download to finish.** Click the file name at the bottom or top of the browser, or open the **Downloads** folder.
3. **Double-click StockFlow-Setup.exe.**
4. **If a blue window says "Windows protected your PC":**
   - Click **More info** (small words, in the middle).
   - Click **Run anyway**.
   This is normal for new apps.
5. **If it asks "Do you want to allow this app to make changes?"** click **Yes**.
6. **Follow the screen.** Click **Next**, then **Install**, then **Finish**. It takes about a minute.
7. **StockFlow opens by itself.** You will also see a **StockFlow** icon on your desktop and in the Start menu. Next, [create your account](#create-your-account).

Your data is kept safe when the app updates itself later.

## In the browser

You do not have to install anything. Open this in Chrome or Edge: **https://stockflow-6l3.pages.dev**

Then [create your account](#create-your-account). On a phone, you can tap the browser menu and choose **Add to Home screen** to keep a shortcut.

## Create your account

You do this only once. Use the same email and password on your phone, computer and browser. All of them will show the same shop.

1. **Open StockFlow.** Check you are connected to the internet. The first time needs it. After that the app also works without internet.
2. **The app opens in the language of your phone or computer.** You can switch between **English** and **বাংলা** any time later in **Settings**.
3. **Tap "New here? Create an account".**
4. **Type your Email.** Use an email you can open (for example Gmail). You will need it if you forget your password.
5. **Type a Password.** It must be at least **6 letters or numbers**. Tap the little eye to see what you typed. **Write the password on paper and keep it safe.**
6. **Tap Sign up.**
7. **Set up your business.** Fill in:
   - **Business name**: your shop's name
   - **Owner name**: your name
   - **Mobile number**
   - **Business type**: choose the one closest to your shop. You can choose this **only once**, so choose carefully.
   - Other boxes (BIN, trade licence, address, branches) are optional. You can fill them later.
8. **Tap Finish setup.**
9. **Wait for approval.** The screen says **"Waiting for approval"**. The StockFlow team approves new shops. When you are told you are approved, tap **Check again**.
10. **Your 15-day free trial starts.** Start by adding a product, then try a sale.

### Forgot your password?

On the sign-in screen tap **Forgot password?**, type your email, and open the email we send you. Tap the link in it and choose a new password.

### Sign in on another phone or computer

Install the app there, tap **Sign in** (not Sign up), and type the same email and password. Your shop appears.

## If something goes wrong

- **"You need an internet connection the first time you sign in":** connect to Wi-Fi or mobile data and try again.
- **Wrong password:** use **Forgot password?**.
- **The app says "Waiting for approval" for a long time:** open **Help & support** and write to us, or ask the person who gave you the app.
- **Cannot install on Android:** make sure there is some free space on your phone (at least 300 MB) and repeat step 4 above.
- **Anything else:** open **Help & support** inside the app and send us a message. We reply there.
